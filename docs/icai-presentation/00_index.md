# ICAI 2026 Presentation — Content Index

Source: `IEEE/jaime.tex`. This folder breaks that paper into presentation-ready
pieces. Read `01_paper_metadata.md` first, then follow the numbered order;
`10_slide_outline_suggested.md` ties everything into an actual slide sequence.

- `01_paper_metadata.md` — title, authors, venue, one-paragraph summary, contribution list
- `02_motivation_related_work.md` — the problem statement and prior work (Introduction + Related Work)
- `03_methodology.md` — the 48-architecture grid, datasets, training protocol, statistics
- `04_results_by_condition.md` — the five experimental conditions, numbers extracted from the tables
- `05_accent_gender_filterbank.md` — the two secondary studies (accent/gender, filter-bank scale)
- `06_key_findings_conclusions.md` — the five conclusions, stated the way the paper states them
- `07_limitations_future_work.md` — caveats already in the paper, and how they connect to MARSA
- `08_glossary.md` — every technical term a non-specialist audience will trip on
- `09_tables_figures_inventory.md` — every table/figure with label, content, and a slide-visual suggestion
- `10_slide_outline_suggested.md` — a proposed slide-by-slide structure for an ICAI talk

Nothing here is invented. Every number is copied from `jaime.tex` verbatim,
with its source line noted where it matters for a claim you'll say out loud.
