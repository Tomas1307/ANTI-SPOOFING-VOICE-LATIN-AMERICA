Objectives

One goal, four pillars

Design and implement HABLA 2.0: a modernized, multi-condition voice anti-spoofing corpus and benchmark for Latin American Spanish, built on four pillars — modern full-synthesis attacks, a partial-spoof corpus with boundary jitter, multi-condition acoustic augmentation, and cross-corpus benchmarking.

Pillar 1
Full-synthesis attacks
6 neural TTS systems (Fish Speech, Qwen3, OmniVoice, Chatterbox, OpenVoice, OuteTTS) replace the legacy GAN attacks.
Mostly done
Pillar 2 new
Partial spoof
Word-level splicing of 1–3 cloned words (W1/W2/W3) plus boundary-jitter adversarial augmentation. First in Spanish.
Validated
Pillar 3
Acoustic augmentation
Multi-condition degradation: 60% RIR+Noise / 30% Codec / 10% RawBoost, for real-world robustness.
Pending
Pillar 4
Cross-corpus benchmarking
SSL detectors (wav2vec 2.0 / WavLM) over full + partial spoof; evaluate vs ASVspoof, HISPASpoof, PartialSpoof.
