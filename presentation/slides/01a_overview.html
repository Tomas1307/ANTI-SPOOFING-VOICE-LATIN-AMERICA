Overview

Eight pipelines

All share the same architecture — Facade orchestrator, Strategy steps, Pydantic schemas, loguru logging. Six distinct TTS codec architectures ensure the detector cannot overfit to a single synthesis fingerprint. Plus a novel word-level partial spoof pipeline.
