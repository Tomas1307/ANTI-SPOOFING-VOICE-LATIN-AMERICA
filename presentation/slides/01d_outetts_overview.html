OuteTTS 1.0 0.6B (Qwen3 + DAC)

LLM-based TTS using a Qwen3 0.6B backbone with DAC audio codec. Supports 14 languages including Spanish, trained on MLS and Common Voice. Autoregressive generation yields high audio quality at the cost of speed. Apache 2.0 license.
