Attack Pipeline — OmniVoice (k2-fsa)

OmniVoice: 646-language diffusion TTS with reference-bleed handling

Diffusion language model from k2-fsa supporting 646 languages, with 27,559 hours of Spanish training data — the largest Spanish coverage in any open zero-shot TTS we evaluated. Required custom post-processing because diffusion sampling occasionally bleeds the reference voice into the leading audio frames in a way that ASR-based detection cannot see.

Reference voice bleed: an artifact ASR cannot see

Diffusion sampling occasionally emits a fragment of the reference voice (200–600 ms) before the prompt content begins. The fragment is sub-syllabic, so Parakeet TDT does not transcribe it — WER stays at 0.0 even though the audio is clearly contaminated. Word-alignment-based prefix detectors (used for Qwen3-TTS hallucinated words) are blind to this failure mode.

Validation result. 100 % recall on bleed detection across 12 trials (initial + 5 retries on 2 affected samples). Final 4 samples passed cleanly. Two samples from one speaker (arf_00295) bled deterministically across all 6 generation attempts — reveals a model-level failure mode tied to specific reference clips that retries alone cannot fix. Investigation of reference selection is the open follow-up.
