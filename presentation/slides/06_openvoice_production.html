Lowest speaker similarity. OpenVoice's tone-color conversion at tau=0.3 only partially transfers speaker characteristics (0.394 mean), less than half of Qwen's 0.720. The MeloTTS base voice "bleeds through." However, it achieves the lowest CER (0.45%) and highest consistency (lowest std on NISQA). For anti-spoofing, OpenVoice attacks are the easiest for embedding-based detectors to catch.
