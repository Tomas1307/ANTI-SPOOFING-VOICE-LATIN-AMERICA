Pipeline characteristics

Architecture
GPT-style autoregressive TTS with CFG (classifier-free guidance). Generates EnCodec tokens then decodes via Vocos vocoder.
Voice cloning
Zero-shot via 15s reference audio + speaker embedding. No fine-tuning required. Watermark removal enabled.

Observations so far

RTF 31-45x is prohibitive. A 5-second utterance takes 150-225 seconds to generate. At this rate, the full 35,927 samples require ~31 days of continuous GPU time. Chatterbox is the slowest of all 5 pipelines by a wide margin (FishGram: 2-3x, Qwen: 3-5x, OpenVoice: 0.07-0.10x).
Quality expectations: Resemble.ai reports high naturalness in English. Spanish quality is unvalidated until the run completes. Early samples show reasonable intelligibility but speaker similarity is TBD.
