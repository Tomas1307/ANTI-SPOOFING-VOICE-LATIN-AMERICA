Lower speaker similarity than other pipelines. OuteTTS's 0.462 mean ECAPA SIM is well below Qwen (0.720) and FishGram (0.602), comparable to OpenVoice (0.394). The WavTokenizer-based reproduction preserves linguistic content well (NISQA 4.45 -- second only to FishGram) but speaker timbre transfer is weaker.
