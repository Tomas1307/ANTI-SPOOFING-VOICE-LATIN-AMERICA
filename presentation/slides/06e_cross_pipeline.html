Production-scale findings. OmniVoice achieves the lowest WER (1.06%) and CER (0.30%) of the suite at 93.9% pass rate — competitive with FishGram on quality, with stronger speaker fidelity than FishGram (0.686 vs 0.602). Qwen3-TTS remains the most dangerous attack by speaker similarity (0.720). FishGram still leads on pass rate (95.2%) and NISQA (4.571). OpenVoice and OuteTTS have the weakest voice cloning (SIM 0.394 and 0.462) — easiest for embedding-based detectors. The 6 architectures span 200M to 4B parameters and 5 distinct synthesis paradigms (VQGAN, LM+codec, VITS-style, flow-matching, LM+wavtokenizer, diffusion LM) — the diversity ensures detectors cannot overfit to a single synthesis fingerprint.
