OuteTTS trade-off: Excellent audio quality (NISQA 4.46-4.76 across validation) and good intelligibility (0% WER on this sample), but moderate speaker similarity (avg 0.47). The 3-second reference window — constrained by the 8,192-token context — limits how much speaker identity can be captured. Generation is slow (~6x RTF) due to autoregressive LLM decoding. Still, it represents a novel LLM+codec architecture absent from LRLSpoof and HispaSpoof.
