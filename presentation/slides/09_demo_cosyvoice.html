CosyVoice 2 (0.5B, Conditional Flow Matching) was evaluated as a potential attack system. Despite near real-time generation speed (RTF 0.77-0.99), it does not support Spanish. The model generates Chinese-sounding audio when given Spanish text input. Dropped from the attack suite.
