SpeechFake: Multilingual Deepfake Dataset

3+ million deepfake samples across 46 languages from 40 TTS tools. Published at ACL 2025 (arXiv:2507.21463). Spanish is one of 9 primary languages. Bonafide sourced from Mozilla CommonVoice — same as our text pipeline.
