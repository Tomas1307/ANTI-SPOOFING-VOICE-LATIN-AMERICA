Related Work — HISPASpoof

HISPASpoof: Spanish Speech Forensics

535,687 speech signals across 6 Spanish accents and 6 TTS systems. From Purdue VIPER Lab (Risques et al., arXiv:2509.09155). The first large-scale Spanish-specific anti-spoofing dataset — and the closest related work to our thesis.

Critical finding: English detectors fail on Spanish

DetectorASVspoof (English)HISPASpoof (Spanish)
LFCC+GMM 3.59% EER 42.71% EER
Wav2Vec2-AASIST 0.27% EER 19.92% EER
PaSST 4.77% EER 32.14% EER
Language gap: Best English detector (0.27% EER) jumps to 19.92% on Spanish. This validates our entire thesis premise — language-specific anti-spoofing research is essential.

Key differentiators. (1) 65x more speakers (1,567 vs 24). (2) Qwen3-TTS and OuteTTS are absent from HISPASpoof. (3) Partial spoof is entirely novel for Spanish. (4) We include Venezuelan and Puerto Rican accents; they include Peninsular. (5) Our per-sample quality validation enables fine-grained attack analysis beyond EER.
