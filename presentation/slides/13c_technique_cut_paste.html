Splice Technique 1 / 7 — 10% of dataset

Direct cut-paste

Hard concatenation at zero-crossings with no overlap or fading. The simplest and most forensically detectable method.

No overlap 10% of splices
Method
Abrupt concatenation at the splice point. Overlap = 0 ms. No fade curves applied. Bonafide segment ends, cloned segment begins immediately.
Source
PartialSpoof (Zhang et al., 2023), HAD (Yi et al., 2021). Both use simple concatenation as their baseline splicing method.
Acoustic effect
Produces energy discontinuities and potential phase jumps at the boundary. Even with zero-crossing alignment, spectral differences between segments create a measurable forensic trace. Most detectable technique — Negroni et al. achieves 6.16% EER with zero training.
Why include it?
Forces detectors to handle the easiest case. If a model cannot detect cut-paste splices, it has no chance with smoother methods. Serves as the forensic floor.
