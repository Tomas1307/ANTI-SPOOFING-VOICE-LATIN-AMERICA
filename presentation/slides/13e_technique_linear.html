Splice Technique 3 / 7 — 15% of dataset

Linear crossfade

The simplest crossfade: a straight diagonal blend. Equal-gain but with a perceptual loudness dip at the midpoint.

Equal-gain 15% of splices
Formula
fade_in(t) = t
fade_out(t) = 1 - t
Source
LlamaPartialSpoof (Luong et al., ICASSP 2025) — one of 5 crossfade functions randomly assigned per splice. Also the baseline in Negroni et al.'s "triangular window" analysis.
Acoustic effect
Constant total amplitude (in + out = 1.0), but perceived loudness dips at t=0.5 because power = 0.5 + 0.5 = 0.5 (half of original). This "loudness hole" is the main perceptual artifact and the main forensic signature of linear crossfades.
