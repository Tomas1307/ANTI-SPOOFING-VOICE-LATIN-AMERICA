Partial Spoof — Summary

Technique distribution & literature gaps

Seven techniques with varied proportions produce a forensically diverse dataset. Each splice independently draws its method and overlap duration, preventing detectors from learning a single crossfade fingerprint.

Design rationale: Equal proportions for the two SOTA methods (OLA Hanning 20%, Cosine 20%). Lower proportions for extreme cases (cut-paste 10%, log 10%, parabola 10%). LlamaPartialSpoof assigns 30-80ms uniformly — we follow this anchor.

Unsolved problems (thesis contributions)

1. Zero-gap boundary policy
No pipeline specifies behavior when adjacent cloned words leave <30ms of bonafide margin. Five strategies exist; none is attested in any paper.
2. F0 discontinuity
No partial-spoof paper reports pitch discontinuity at splice boundaries. Publishing F0 histograms at MFA word boundaries would be novel.
3. Crossfade duration vs EER
LlamaPartialSpoof's 30-80ms range is the only anchor. No paper maps overlap length to detection performance — a tractable publishable experiment.
4. No source code released
None of the 4 canonical pipelines (PartialSpoof, LlamaPartialSpoof, HAD, HQ-MPSD) releases their splicer code. Our pipeline is fully reproducible.
