GATE 1 — Clone Similarity
Method
ECAPA-TDNN (192-d embedding) cosine similarity between the bonafide reference speaker and the generated TTS clone. Applied before alignment and splicing to avoid wasting compute on bad clones.

GATE 2 — Valley Score Word Selection
Method
For each word boundary, measure how deep the energy valley is. Only words at naturally quiet boundaries are eligible for replacement. This prevents splicing at fluid speech points where no natural cut exists.
Formula
score = min_rms / avg_rms
Computed in 5ms frames within ±100ms of the boundary. 0.0 = perfect silence. 1.0 = no valley. Threshold: 0.65 — words above are ineligible.
Additional Eligibility Constraints
duration ≥ 200ms  — no short function words
stretch ∈ [0.80, 1.25] — time-stretch within ±25%
non-adjacent     — selected words ≥ 2 apart
