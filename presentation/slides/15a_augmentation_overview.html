Methodology — Data Augmentation

Channel & device augmentation: training for the conditions we have not seen

After the corpus is built (bonafide + every TTS attack, in ASVspoof2019 LA format), each clip is passed through a stochastic augmentation layer before a detector ever sees it. The goal is robustness, not realism: aggressive, even non-physical perturbations regularize the detector and expand the training distribution so it generalizes to unseen codecs, rooms and devices — rather than overfitting a single clean recording condition.

Why not "realistic only"? A detector trained on a single clean condition is brittle to distribution shift — new TTS, new phones, new codecs land outside its experience. Offline augmentation (RIR+Noise + Codec) plus the deliberately non-physical RawBoost applied at training-time forces the model to rely on the synthesis artifact rather than the recording condition. The one hard constraint: augmentation must never be so destructive that it erases the spoofing cue itself.
