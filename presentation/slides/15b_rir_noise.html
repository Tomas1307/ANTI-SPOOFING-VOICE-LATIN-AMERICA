Augmentation — Layer 1: RIR + Noise

The acoustic environment: reverberation and background noise

Reverberation is applied by convolving the clip with a Room Impulse Response from RIRS_NOISES (Ko et al., ICASSP 2017), stratified by room size (small / medium / large). Background noise is drawn from MUSAN — ambient noise, music, or babble (background talkers) — and mixed at a randomly sampled SNR. The two stack to model a speaker in a real room over a real microphone.
