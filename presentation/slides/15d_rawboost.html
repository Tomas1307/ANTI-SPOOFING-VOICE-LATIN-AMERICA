Augmentation — Layer 3: RawBoost

RawBoost: a deliberately non-physical regularizer

RawBoost (Tak et al., ICASSP 2022) applies three stochastic distortions directly to the waveform. It does not try to mimic any single real device — that is the point. By scrambling device and channel cues in a controlled, randomized way, it forces the detector to key on the synthesis artifact instead of a recording fingerprint. We use the official algorithm (the three components in series / parallel), not an approximation.

LnL convolutive
linear + non-linear
Randomized multiband notch FIRs plus a Hammerstein polynomial — arbitrary spectral coloration and harmonic distortion.
ISD additive
impulsive, signal-dependent
Impulse-like perturbations whose amplitude scales with the local signal — models burst electronic interference.
SSI additive
stationary, signal-independent
White noise colored by a random FIR, added at a sampled SNR — a stationary noise floor.

It should sound harsh. Unlike RIR and codec, RawBoost is not meant to sound like a real channel — it is an adversarial spread of the training distribution. Its strength comes precisely from being out-of-distribution for any one device.
