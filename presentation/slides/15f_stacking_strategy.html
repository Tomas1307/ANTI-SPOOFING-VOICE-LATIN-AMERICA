Augmentation — Redesign Decision

New distribution: stacking gate + RawBoost moved to training-time

Three design changes locked before the augmentation run. Each decision below has a concrete technical rationale and is validated in code.

Decision 1 — RawBoost
10% offline → training-time
Pre-baking RawBoost inflates disk corpus by 10% without adding information. Applied on-the-fly during training instead: free augmentation, no storage cost, composable with the trainer’s data-loading loop.
Decision 2 — Codec weight
30% → 40%
The freed 10% from RawBoost is absorbed by Codec. Telephone and VoIP degradation is the dominant real-world channel for voice attacks; doubling its presence versus RIR-only improves coverage without new augmenters.
Decision 3 — Stacking gate
one aug/clip → 40% stacked
40% of augmented clips receive both RIR+Noise then Codec in sequence. Models phone calls in a noisy room — the most common attack vector. The remaining 60% get a single augmentation as before.
